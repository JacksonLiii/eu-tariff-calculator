Check your email for your License Key

Creem, our payment provider, sends your License Key by email automatically — it usually arrives within a couple of minutes. It goes to the address you entered at checkout.

Don't see it? Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder before anything else — automated license emails land there more often than you'd expect. Searching your mailbox for Creem or EU Tariff is the fastest way to find it.

Activate Pro in three steps

  1. Copy your License Key from the email. Select the whole key and copy it. Avoid picking up any spaces before or after it.
  2. Open the extension. Click the EU Tariff & Landed Cost Calculator icon in your Chrome toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon and pick it from the list — you can pin it there to keep it handy.
  3. Paste the key and activate. At the bottom of the popup, click Already purchased? Activate with a License Key, paste your key into the input, then click Activate. You'll see Activated! Pro is now unlocked. and the daily limit disappears right away.

Activation is stored on your device, so you only need to do this once per browser.
