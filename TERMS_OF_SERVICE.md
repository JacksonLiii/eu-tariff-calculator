## 1. What the extension is

The extension is an **estimation tool**. It calculates an estimated import cost for a shipment sent to a European Union country, based on:

- a declared value you enter,
- the number of distinct HS6 product categories you enter, and
- a destination EU country you select.

From these inputs it estimates customs duty, VAT, a total landed cost, and the added cost as a percentage of declared value. All calculation happens locally in your browser, using a static table of EU VAT rates and duty parameters bundled inside the extension.

The extension is not a customs broker, not a filing service, and not a tax service. It does not submit anything to any customs authority on your behalf.

## 2. Not official customs or tax advice

**This is the most important term in this document.**

The figures the extension produces are estimates for reference and planning purposes only. They do not constitute official customs advice, tax advice, or legal advice, and they are not a guarantee of accuracy.

Duty and VAT rules change, are applied differently by different member states, and depend on facts the extension does not know about your shipment. In particular:

- The VAT rates and duty parameters bundled in the extension are maintained manually and may be out of date at the moment you use them. The extension displays a "last verified" date — treat it as the actual currency of the data.
- A customs clearance handling fee (approximately €2 per package) is expected to take effect around November 2026. Until it is confirmed active, it is shown separately and is **not** included in the totals.
- Only a limited set of destination countries is supported.

Always verify against official sources, or consult a qualified customs or tax professional, before making business decisions. **Do not rely on the extension's output as the sole basis for pricing, quoting, or declaring a shipment.**
